Accessibility Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Zuhd Guidance is committed to making Islamic knowledge accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim for our website and content to be usable by the widest possible audience.

1. Our Commitment

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, and we continually review our website to improve its accessibility.

2. Measures We Take

  • Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Readable font sizes and sufficient colour contrast.
  • A responsive layout that works across devices and screen sizes.
  • Keyboard-navigable menus and controls where possible.
  • Captions or descriptive titles for video content where available.

3. Known Limitations

Some older or third-party embedded content (such as social media feeds and external video players) may not fully meet accessibility standards. We work with our providers to improve this over time.

4. Feedback & Assistance

If you experience any difficulty accessing part of our website, or would like content provided in an alternative format, please contact us at info@zuhd.au. We will do our best to assist you promptly.
